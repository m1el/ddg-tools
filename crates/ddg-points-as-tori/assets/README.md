cube map used by the demo renderer of the authors' reference implementation
(`demo/assets/` of `points-as-tori`, MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Nicole Feng). Copied unchanged
(64 x 64, 8-bit RGB, 16 KB in all). The licence notice is `LICENSE-points-as-tori-MIT.txt`.
They are embedded by `src/render/cubemap.rs` and are the environment of the IBL-GGX term.
